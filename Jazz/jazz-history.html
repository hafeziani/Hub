A Schematic History of Jazz

An era-by-era map of how jazz evolved, who drove each shift, and the albums worth hearing to understand it. Jazz didn't move in a straight line — styles overlapped, coexisted, and looped back on each other — but this rough chronology is the standard way historians and musicians frame it.

A 15-Album Starter Path

If you're building a jazz collection from scratch, listened to roughly in this order this gives a working sense of the whole arc.

A note on sourcing: this overview reflects widely agreed-upon jazz-historical consensus (the kind found in standard references like Ted Gioia's The History of Jazz or the Ken Burns Jazz documentary), not a single disputed reading — but as with any genre history, specialists will quibble over emphasis and boundaries between eras.
